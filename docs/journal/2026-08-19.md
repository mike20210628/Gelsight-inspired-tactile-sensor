## Design Decisions

I decided that the sensor should be built as an integrated modular box with the elastomer exposed as the contact surface. The camera will remain inside the enclosure and observe the elastomer from behind, while RGB illumination will be positioned around the sensing area.

A replaceable elastomer cartridge appears to be more suitable than permanently attaching the silicone to the housing because several elastomer thicknesses and coating methods still need to be evaluated.

I also decided that the camera should not be permanently fixed at one distance during the initial assembly. Instead, the camera mount should allow a small amount of vertical adjustment and then be mechanically locked once the best focus and field of view are established.

## Observations

The approximately 2 mm silicone sample appeared significantly more transparent than the thicker approximately 5.7 mm sample. This suggests that optical transmission may become an important limitation when selecting the final elastomer thickness.

However, the 2017 GelSight paper also uses thin elastomer sheets in its compact fingertip designs, so a thinner elastomer may still be suitable for the prototype.

The cured silicone is very soft and has a slightly tacky, high-friction surface. Because of this softness, supporting the elastomer only around its edges may cause the center to sag, so a transparent support plate may be necessary.

## Problems / Questions

* The final elastomer thickness has not yet been selected.
* The current silicone is not as optically clear as the material shown in the original GelSight system.
* A suitable reflective coating still needs to be selected and tested for compatibility with the platinum-cure silicone.
* The method for applying and spacing the black optical markers still needs to be determined.
* A transparent support material must be selected that is mechanically strong enough for repeated contact while remaining optically clear.
* The enclosure dimensions and exact positions of the LEDs, camera, glass support, and elastomer cartridge still need to be finalized.
